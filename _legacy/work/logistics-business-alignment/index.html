CASE 04 / BUSINESS DESIGN · STAKEHOLDER ALIGNMENT

Creating shared direction for a new logistics business.

A logistics business established by Braskem was shaping how it would manage vessels, work with its founding client and offer services to external customers. The early challenge was to bring leadership and teams into the same conversation about how the business would operate.

Challenge

Establish how a new logistics business will work across teams and customers.

Contribution

Bring leadership and operational teams into a shared strategy conversation.

Delivered

Ownership maps, strategic analysis and value-versus-effort priorities.

CONTEXT

Braskem · New logistics business

MY FOCUS

Strategy, discovery & facilitation

SCOPE

Leadership & cross-functional alignment

TIMEFRAME

8-hour workshop · Netherlands

At a glanceDecisions & project materialOutputs & outcomes

THE BUSINESS QUESTION

How should a new business connect its teams, customer relationships and growth priorities?

My contribution

The work brought business strategy into a collaborative setting: surfacing roadblocks and dependencies, examining customer needs, separating certainties from assumptions, and organising opportunities by value and effort.

Collaboration

14 participants, including leadership and representatives from Sales, Product Management, Marketing and Logistics.

01 / DECISION

Make dependencies and ownership visible

Expose the responsibilities and dependencies that the business needs to resolve.

Read the decision context ＋

Cross-functional groups explored what each area did, where it depended on others and which roadblocks affected the business. Ownership mapping made relationships and questions visible at an early stage, when the operating model was still being established.

Growth required a shared understanding of how the organisation would work.

02 / DECISION

Connect the offer to customers and uncertainty

Separate customer needs, known constraints and untested assumptions.

Read the decision context ＋

Value Proposition Canvas work explored customer activities, pains and gains. SWOT supported discussion of the existing business, while Certainties, Assumptions and Questions helped the growth group expose what still needed to be tested.

Different levels of certainty called for different next steps.

03 / DECISION

Turn the conversation into priorities

Organise the opportunities into clearer next decisions.

Read the decision context ＋

A value-versus-effort matrix helped organise opportunities and discuss quick wins alongside longer-term work. The outputs provided a basis for the next internal planning steps, rather than a claim that the full strategy had already been implemented.

Alignment is useful when it makes the next decisions clearer.

OUTPUTS & OUTCOMES

What the work made possible.

The workshop produced ownership and roadblock maps, value proposition work, strategic analysis and a prioritised set of opportunities. Its contribution was an early foundation for leadership and team alignment; subsequent commercial growth is not quantified in this case.

What I take forward

The workshop was a means to an organisational conversation. The larger design challenge was how a new business would coordinate its areas and relationships as its service offer evolved.
