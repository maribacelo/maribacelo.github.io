CASE 05 / RESEARCH · COMMERCIAL STRATEGY

Helping a consultancy decide where to focus its commercial effort.

A consultancy needed to decide which prospective customers to focus on for sales outreach and marketing campaigns. The research connected commercial history with stakeholder perspectives to define better-fit company profiles and buyer personas.

Challenge

Choose which prospects deserve sales and marketing effort.

Contribution

Combine commercial history, interviews and market analysis.

Delivered

Ideal, acceptable and incompatible profiles, plus buyer personas.

CONTEXT

Consultancy · Client name withheld

MY FOCUS

CX, research, data analysis & strategy

SCOPE

Customer profiling & prospecting priorities

TIMEFRAME

4-week project

At a glanceDecisions & project materialOutputs & outcomes

THE BUSINESS QUESTION

Which prospects are the best fit for the consultancy, and what should shape outreach to them?

My contribution

My role brought together customer experience, data analysis, research and strategy. Working with a multidisciplinary team, the project translated business information and interviews into customer profiles and buyer perspectives for commercial planning.

Collaboration

Innovation Strategist, CX Strategist, UX Researcher, Marketing Analyst and a trainee.

01 / DECISION

Connect commercial history with market context

Use historical business and market context to question future customer fit.

Read the decision context ＋

The team reviewed business transaction data and market information to understand historical negotiations, service demand and potential customer segments. Interviews with internal leaders explored commercial priorities and operational constraints.

Past customers were evidence to examine, rather than the automatic definition of future customers.

02 / DECISION

Understand the buyer and refine the fit

Bring company attributes and buyer needs into the same analysis.

Read the decision context ＋

Client and prospect interviews explored needs and buying considerations. Gartner consultant input helped refine the analysis. The work brought company attributes together with operational, technological and behavioural considerations.

Company fit and the people involved in buying were complementary views.

03 / DECISION

Make the research useful for prospecting

Translate the findings into profiles that sales and marketing can use.

Read the decision context ＋

The project defined ideal, acceptable and incompatible company profiles, alongside buyer personas. The framework gave the consultancy a way to discuss which prospects deserved effort and how communication could respond to their context.

The value of a profile is in the choices it helps a team make.
